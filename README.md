# About Me:
Hi, I’m Divine. I’m a Computer Science student who originally got into coding through art, and ended up staying because I genuinely love creating things. Websites, small tools, software, anything tangible that people can see, use, or interact with. I like the idea that something I build can exist outside my head and actually impact someone else.<br><br>I started with HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and JavaScript before exploring Python, C and SQL through university projects. One of my first major projects was a Python vocabulary builder, which helped me better understand how differnet parts of an application work togther and sparked a deeper interest in software development.<br><br>Most of my experience so far comes from univeristy projects, where I've worked on everything from interactive applications to web development and databases. I enjoy the process of turning an idea into something functional, even when that involves a lot of trial and error, debugging and figuring out why someting that worked five minutes ago has suddenly stopped working<br><br>I'm particularly interested in software and web development, although I'm still exploring other areas of technology and figuring out what kind of developer I want to become. I'd like to experiment with more tools and frameworks, inculding React, and continue building projects that challenge me creatively and technically<br><br>IOutside of coding, I enjoy drawing, playing guitar, learning piano and creative writing. I'm also a big fan of worldbuilding, and I have a long-term creative project that I'd love to bring to life through a website and other interactive experiences<br><br>I’m still learning, experimenting and finding my direction, but I care about what I create and enjoy seeing ideas gradually become something real.
